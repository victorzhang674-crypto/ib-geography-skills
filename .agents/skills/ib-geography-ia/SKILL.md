# IB Geography Internal Assessment

Use the first-assessment-2019 syllabus, updated November 2022. The IA is one individual fieldwork report of at most 2,500 words. It uses the same six criteria at SL and HL (/25), with weights of 25% at SL and 20% at HL. The guide allocates about 20 hours.

The investigation must address a geographical question at a local scale, link to a specific syllabus topic or subtopic, and be based on sufficient first-hand data collected by the student in the field. Referenced secondary material can play a small supporting role. Students can prepare and collect data together. The methods justification, analysis, conclusions and final text are individual work.

## Plan and coach the writing

1. Identify the student's stage, site, available data and deadline where relevant. Read [the fieldwork and writing guide](references/writing.md). If the investigation is still open, compare feasible questions and map the selected question to site access, measurable variables, sampling, a collection protocol and an analysis plan. With existing data, check what the actual sampling design can answer before choosing tests or reshaping the question.
2. Give a report outline mapped to A–F, with figure and evidence prompts. Prioritize interpretation of primary data and links to geographic theory, the location and the method. Help the student turn their observations into their own analysis through questions, an annotated outline, draft feedback or brief clearly labeled practice examples.
3. Preserve student authorship. Keep assessed-report help at planning, technique explanations and feedback. Use real observations and clearly distinguish illustrative data from fieldwork evidence.

## Assess a draft

Read [the A–F rubric](references/rubric.md). Ask for relevant report pages, maps and figures when needed. Check the word count and identify what falls within the assessable text. Award each criterion an independent best-fit integer and cite its evidence by section, figure or short excerpt. Explain close decisions using the neighboring descriptor and identify an actionable revision. Treat missing material in a partial draft as unassessed. Apply the zero descriptor when a criterion in the complete assessable submission has no evidence meeting the lowest descriptor.

Return a criterion breakdown, a total /25 when the whole report can be assessed, and two or three prioritized revisions tied to specific evidence. Label scores as estimates. For a plan, flag what evidence the student still needs to collect or create for each criterion.
