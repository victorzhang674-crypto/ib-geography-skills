# IB Geography Paper 1

Use the IB DP Geography syllabus for first assessment in 2019, updated November 2022. Each optional theme is worth 20 marks. Answer the compulsory stimulus-related parts (/10), then choose one of the two extended responses for that same theme (/10). The essay may go beyond the stimulus. SL answers two themes in 1 h 30 min (/40). HL answers three in 2 h 15 min (/60). The paper is 35% at both levels.

The seven options are A Freshwater, B Oceans and coastal margins, C Extreme environments, D Geophysical hazards, E Leisure, tourism and sport, F Food and health, and G Urban environments. Ask which options the student studies when planning a whole paper. A single question can be marked or planned without knowing the level if its scope and mark allocation are clear.

## Mark a response

1. Identify the option, exact question, command term and mark allocation. Obtain the answer, legible stimulus or resource booklet where needed, and matching question-specific markscheme when available. Match session, version and question number before using it.
2. Read [the marking reference](references/marking.md). Mark short parts analytically and the essay with one best-fit /10 mark, informed by the question-specific scheme. If the scheme is unavailable, label the estimate provisional and explain what earns credit under a reasonable reading of the question. Leave stimulus-dependent marks unassessed if the source is missing or unreadable.
3. For each assessed part, give the mark and maximum, evidence that earned credit, and a specific improvement. For the essay, identify the band and why the adjacent band fits less well. Sum the scope actually assessed. Distinguish an excerpt with unavailable parts from a confirmed blank in a full script. Avoid predicting a 1–7 grade without matching boundaries and the other component results.

## Plan or write a practice answer

- For stimulus parts, read the key, axes, units and scale before interpreting. For a description, specify the pattern and a source detail where relevant. For an explanation, show the geographical process and its effect. Match the number and depth of points to the command term, mark allocation and question wording.
- For the essay, identify the precise claim, place or scale and qualifiers. Plan evidence-led paragraphs that link a located example to a mechanism, then weigh competing explanations, exceptions or stakeholder perspectives as the question requires. End with a judgment that answers the prompt and follows from the evidence. Annotate any diagram the argument relies on.
- In a timed plan, allow about 45 minutes per theme. Give a practice split suited to the student. When producing a practice prompt or mock markscheme, label it as original. Use substantiated case facts or clearly marked placeholders.
