# IB Geography Paper 3

Use the first-assessment-2019 syllabus, updated November 2022. HL students have 1 hour to choose one of three questions and answer both parts of that question. Part (a) is /12 and part (b) is /16, for /28 in total and 20% of the HL course grade.

The HL extension covers Power, places and networks, Human development and diversity, and Global risks and resilience. Questions can draw across these units and assume knowledge of the core.

## Mark a response

1. Get the exact question, identify part (a) or (b), and use the matching session and version markscheme if available. Read [the two band scales](references/marking.md). Use the relevant question-specific guidance alongside the bands. Mark independently with one best-fit integer per part. Label estimates based on the general bands alone as provisional.
2. In (a), look for coverage of all demands, detailed and applied evidence, explained geographical relationships and coherent organization. In (b), also assess how the answer connects extension topics, synthesizes and evaluates evidence, and justifies its perspective.
3. State the band, exact mark, evidence from the response and a concrete revision that could improve it. Explain close decisions against the neighboring band. Report /28 only for a complete paired assessment, treating a confirmed omitted part as blank and a part absent from the materials as unassessed. Keep 1–7 grade predictions separate from the raw paper marks.

## Plan or write a practice answer

- **(a):** Split the question into its required processes, relationships or categories. Give each a claim and a geographically located example. Explain how the example demonstrates the process, including relevant actors and scales, and connect the paragraphs. Close by tying the explanation back to the question. Formal evaluation is unnecessary under the (a) bands.
- **(b):** Define the issue, scale and judgment criteria early. Use a claim, located evidence, causal explanation and evaluation for each major strand. Explain connections between relevant extension topics. Weigh alternative explanations or perspectives, and compare how outcomes vary with place, scale, actors or time where relevant. Qualify the overall judgment by the certainty and scope of the evidence.
- For practice, one possible split is 5 minutes to select and plan, 23 minutes for (a), 29 minutes for (b) and 3 minutes to check. Label any mock prompt or indicative answer as original. Use verified case details or mark placeholders.
