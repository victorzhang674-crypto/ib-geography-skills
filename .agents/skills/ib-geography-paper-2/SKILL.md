# IB Geography Paper 2

Use the first-assessment-2019 syllabus, updated November 2022. SL and HL sit the same 1 h 15 min paper, worth 50 marks. It is 40% at SL and 25% at HL.

| Section | Task | Marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Answer all three structured questions, one per core unit. | 30 |
| B | Answer the compulsory visual stimulus question, which can draw on any core unit. | 10 |
| C | Choose one of two extended-response questions. A question may integrate core units. | 10 |

The core units are Changing population, Global climate—vulnerability and resilience, and Global resource consumption and security.

## Mark a response

1. Identify the exact prompts, mark allocations, answer, and any legible stimulus or resource booklet. Use the question-specific markscheme for the correct session, version and question when available.
2. Read [the marking reference](references/marking.md). Mark A and B analytically. Award Section C one holistic /10 mark using the markbands and the question-specific guidance. Without the scheme, label estimated marks provisional. If missing stimulus prevents checking a part, identify that part and leave it unassessed.
3. Give a breakdown by item and section, with evidence that earned marks and a specific next step. For Section C, explain the best-fit band and what distinguishes the next band. Report the total for the supplied, assessable scope. Distinguish a confirmed blank on a full script from a part that was never supplied. A 1–7 grade requires the relevant boundaries and other component results.

## Plan or write a practice answer

- **A:** Map each subpart to its command term and marks. For multi-point explanations, make each reason distinct and show how it produces the stated outcome. When examples are requested, choose geographically located evidence and connect it to the mechanism.
- **B:** Read the legend, dates, scale, units and denominators. Point to values or spatial relationships from the visual when required, then draw on geographical knowledge if asked to explain. Check whether a trend is absolute, relative or per capita before comparing.
- **C:** Define the scope of the question and form a provisional judgment. Plan paragraphs around mechanisms or factors, each with located evidence and a link to the question. Compare the importance of factors under stated conditions, and connect core units where the question rewards that link. Conclude with a supported answer to the command term.
- For a whole-paper practice plan, offer a split such as 40 minutes for A, 12 for B, 20 for C and 3 for checking. Adapt it to the student. Clearly label original questions, worked responses and practice marking notes. Use verifiable evidence or mark placeholders.
